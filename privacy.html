Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This page explains what information LN Photogrammetry collects through this website, why, and how it's used. This is a small, owner-operated business site, not a data broker; the goal here is to be plain about what actually happens.

Information You Provide

When you submit the quote form, we collect your name, email address, and whatever property details you type in. This is sent to our form processor (Formspree) and used only to respond to your inquiry, schedule work, and communicate about your project. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies from a third-party ad network) for a few basic things:

  • Remembering whether you prefer light or dark mode
  • Remembering that you've dismissed the cookie/storage notice
  • If you arrive via a marketing link (for example, a link with campaign tags), storing which link brought you here so we understand which advertising is working

None of this identifies you personally, and none of it is sold to advertisers. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-Party Services

The 3D model demo on this site is hosted by a third-party viewer (SuperSplat). Our contact form is processed by Formspree. Both may have their own privacy practices for the technical delivery of their service; we don't control those independently, but we don't hand either of them anything beyond what's needed to show you a model or deliver your message to us.

What We Don't Do

We don't run third-party ad trackers, sell visitor data, or share your contact information with anyone outside of running this business.

Your Choices

You can decline local storage by adjusting your browser settings, though this may reset your theme preference each visit. You can request that we delete any information you've submitted through the contact form by reaching out directly.
