Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything most people ask before booking. Call or text if yours isn't here.

What areas do you serve?+

Lake Nona and the greater Orlando area. If you're just outside that range, reach out. Longer drives may carry a small travel add-on.

How long does a capture take?+

Most standard homes take about 30-40 minutes on site for the aerial flight. Larger lots or multiple buildings take longer.

What if it rains or is too windy to fly?+

We reschedule at no charge. Wind and rain both affect flight safety and image quality, so we wait for a clean weather window rather than deliver a lower-quality capture.

Do I need to be at the property during the capture?

Not necessarily, but we do need reliable access arranged ahead of time, whether that's a gate code, lockbox, or someone letting us onto the property.

Can I use the 3D model anywhere I want?+

Yes. The delivered model and shareable link are yours to embed on your listing page, MLS (where supported), and social media.

What's your turnaround time?+

48 hours is typical from capture to delivery. Rush turnaround may be available; ask when you request a quote.

What if something in the model looks off?+

Reach out and we'll take a look. Minor cleanup on our end is part of the service; we want the delivered model to actually be usable for you.
